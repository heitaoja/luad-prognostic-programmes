# Statistical and measurement methods

The dated design and the later extensions are recorded in the [analysis specification](analysis_plan_v2.md). Exact input snapshots, phenotype fields and repository accessions are listed in the [data provenance](data_provenance.md). The full study includes TCGA-LUAD, four GEO microarray series and CPTAC-LUAD. TCGA is the discovery and training source. The other five cohorts are external.

## Participant, endpoint and expression handling

The unit of inference is a patient with a primary LUAD specimen. Link TCGA samples by exact specimen identifier when available and by 12-character participant barcode for unmatched records. Check repeated vials for survival agreement, then retain the first eligible primary vial in barcode order. Repeat TCGA association with the last eligible vial. Link CPTAC expression, clinical data and OS by unique case ID. Convert days to months by dividing by 30.4375 and years by multiplying by 12. Death from any cause is the event. Report OS eligibility, complete clinical cases, deaths and reverse Kaplan–Meier median follow-up. [TCGA flow](../results/tables/01_tcga_participant_flow.csv) · [Cohort audit](../results/tables/01_cohort_audit.csv) · [CPTAC audit](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_audit.csv)

Use log-scale bulk expression with Ensembl or platform probe definitions mapped to gene symbols. For cohort-specific association, select the duplicate feature with the largest cohort mean log expression. For transport, average duplicate features within each specimen before ranking. Select the fixed transport universe using measured and annotated symbols, which yields 17,595 genes for TCGA and GEO. CPTAC lacks ACTL10 and NPBWR1, so fit a separate TCGA reference on the compatible set of 17,593. Minimum Hallmark coverage is 96.5% for both transport universes. [Hallmark coverage](../results/tables/02_gene_coverage.csv) · [Shared list](../results/tables/02_transport_gene_universe.csv) · [CPTAC coverage](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_coverage.csv)

## Transcriptional programmes and scale sensitivities

We define four programmes from 14 Hallmark sets. The proliferation composite combines E2F, G2/M checkpoint and MYC targets V1 and V2. The other programmes represent hypoxia and glycolysis, EMT/stroma, and immune response. For a sample with $G$ genes and descending expression midrank $r_g$, set $v_g=G-r_g+1$. Single-sample gene set enrichment is the difference between the in-set mean of $v_g$ weighted by $v_g^{0.25}$ and the unweighted out-of-set mean, divided by $G$. Association scores standardise components within each cohort, average components with equal weights and standardise the average. [Programme membership](../results/tables/02_axis_definition.csv) · [Hallmark collection](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004)

For the expression-based sensitivity, standardise each measured, nonconstant gene in a programme's union across patients, then standardise the sample mean of these z-scores. The sample-centred variant subtracts the specimen's median across all measured genes first. That operation removes a sample-wide additive log offset, which can approximate a multiplicative library-size effect on TCGA log STAR counts when counts are large. It can also change biological sample-level variation. The within-sample midranks are invariant to a common strictly increasing transformation after feature mapping. [Scoring sensitivity](../results/tables/06_sensitivity.csv)

## Association and evidence synthesis

The main cohort models use one continuous standardised programme with stage, cohort-standardised age and sex in a Cox model. For individual CPTAC estimates use stage I versus II–IV to limit parameters. Score and global Schoenfeld tests, a three-degree-of-freedom natural spline, stage-stratified baselines, stage I–II restriction, alternate TCGA vials and a four-programme joint model assess modelling sensitivity. The TCGA stage I–III results and the GSE31210 recurrence endpoint are exploratory. [Cohort estimates](../results/tables/04_cohort_estimates.csv) · [CPTAC association](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_association.csv) · [Diagnostics](../results/tables/03c_model_diagnostics.csv)

Fit separate cohort log HRs and pool external effects using REML random effects and modified Knapp–Hartung inference with $k-1$ degrees of freedom and a variance multiplier of at least one. Report DL with normal inference as a sensitivity. The original three GEO series contribute 437 complete cases and 158 deaths. The four-array scope contributes 830 and 269. With CPTAC the totals are 935 and 292. In one-stage IPD, retain cohort-standardised scores and age, encode stage I–IV and sex, and estimate a common score slope using a cohort-specific baseline with a stage term, or a cohort-by-stage baseline without a separate stage term. Use Holm correction within each method and scope across the four programmes. [Original array synthesis](../results/tables/04_meta_analysis.csv) · [IPD and five-cohort synthesis](../results/tables/04b_ipd_and_pan_external_synthesis.csv)

## Purity, smoking, drivers and specificity

Match TCGA DNA-derived ABSOLUTE purity by exact specimen. Compare HRs before and after adding purity on the same specimens and report Spearman correlations. Apply the same matched-patient comparison for TCGA pack-years, GSE31210 smoking and alteration group, GSE50081 smoking, and GSE72094 smoking or the four EGFR, KRAS, TP53 and STK11 indicators in separate extended models. Driver subgroup Cox fits in GSE31210 and GSE72094 use the cohort score scales. With event-free stage or sex categories, penalise those design columns using ridge with theta 1 and scaling disabled. Estimate score and age slopes without penalisation. [Purity](../results/tables/03c_purity_sensitivity.csv) · [Smoking and drivers](../results/tables/03d_driver_smoking_adjustment.csv)

For matched random programmes, replace each gene in the programme union one-to-one with another measured gene in the same mean-rank decile, excluding the observed programme union. Map a gene to the same replacement across its component sets to preserve overlap. Use 500 draws per programme in TCGA and 200 in GSE31210 and GSE72094, and estimate upper-tail empirical probabilities with the plus-one correction. The outcome-permutation control shuffles survival time and status together relative to predictors 1,000 times per programme in TCGA. [Matched null](../results/tables/06_random_set_null.csv) · [Outcome permutations](../results/tables/06_permutation_test.csv)

## Molecular benchmark

Use matched complete cases for comparisons with MKI67 expression, E2F and G2/M. Estimate each marker with clinical adjustment and estimate the composite conditional on each marker. CCP-31 is the 31-gene list from the Wistuba LUAD publication. Meta-PCNA uses the 131-gene list from Venet's supplementary table. Resolve historical HGNC aliases using the curated mapping in the code, prioritising the current symbol when present. Audit coverage, correlate scores with the composite, fit each score with clinical adjustment and estimate both coefficients in models containing the composite and each published score. For association, compare ssGSEA with the sample-centred mean gene-wise z-score sensitivity. For the published benchmark, include each scope under REML-mKH, DL and cohort-stratified IPD. These are secondary expression-based comparisons. ## Model transport and baseline recalibration

Fit Cox models in TCGA with stage I–IV, sex with female as reference and age per decade centred at 65. Compare this clinical model with the addition of the proliferation composite, the within-sample MKI67 percentile rank, E2F or G2/M. CCP-31 and meta-PCNA models use ssGSEA or the within-specimen mean log expression standardised to the TCGA reference. Fix the TCGA transforms, coefficients and 36-month cumulative baseline hazard for every external evaluation. For CPTAC, derive both coefficients and transforms in TCGA on the compatible gene subset. [GEO coefficients](../results/tables/05_frozen_coefficients.csv) · [GEO score reference](../results/tables/05_frozen_score_reference.csv) · [CPTAC-compatible coefficients](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_frozen_coefficients.csv) · [CPTAC-compatible score reference](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_score_reference.csv)

Evaluate the frozen linear predictor with Harrell's C and Uno's IPCW C using the survival concordance time weights `n/G2` for Uno. Use the same 1,000 test-patient bootstrap draws for all models in each comparison. The 95% percentile intervals for differences versus the clinical model and Harrell differences between molecular models describe uncertainty conditional on the TCGA fits. [GEO transport](../results/tables/05_incremental_value.csv) · [CPTAC transport](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_transport.csv) · [Published signature transport](../results/tables/05d_published_signature_transport.csv)

Require at least 10 deaths by 36 months and at least 10 patients at risk at 36 months to assess absolute risk. Estimate observed mortality as one minus Kaplan–Meier survival. The Brier score uses inverse test-cohort censoring survival at the left limit of the event time for deaths by the horizon and at 36 months for those observed beyond it. Patients censored by the horizon contribute zero weight. Compute a separate calibration slope from a Cox fit of survival on the frozen linear predictor. For recalibration, fit a Cox baseline with the linear predictor as a fixed offset, extract the cumulative hazard at 36 months with offset zero, then calculate individual risk with the same linear predictor. Reuse the IPC weights for the two Brier estimates. The clinical and composite calibration curves use identical quartile membership before and after the update. The same target data define and evaluate the baseline update. [GEO recalibration](../results/tables/05c_recalibration.csv) · [CPTAC recalibration](../results/tables/05b_rnaseq_recalibration.csv)
