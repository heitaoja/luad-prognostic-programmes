# Interpretation and limitations

1. The primary comparisons describe observational associations with all-cause mortality in lung adenocarcinoma. The available clinical adjustment set is stage, age and sex. Treatment, smoking history, comorbidity and histologic detail were incomplete or heterogeneous across datasets.
2. The four programmes were chosen after TCGA exploration. Results in TCGA are discovery evidence. The original three GEO cohorts and the expanded set give separate estimates. Differences between results from small-k and normal-approximation synthesis affect the precision of the evidence.
3. The RNA measures come from bulk tissue. The subset sensitivity with DNA-derived ABSOLUTE purity addresses one aspect of tumour composition. Purity is available for a subset with exact specimen matches. Cell-type-specific expression and causal mechanisms remain outside this study.
4. The molecular composite and simple markers share biological signal. A conditional estimate can be imprecise when scores are correlated. Assess the paired external prediction contrasts together with the association models.
5. The main external synthesis and model transport use GPL570 or GPL15048 microarrays. The independent CPTAC RNA-seq evaluation uses a TCGA model fitted on the compatible gene list, with 23 deaths and limited precision. Prospective assay and clinical performance remain open.
6. Stage editions, follow-up origin, disease mix and censoring vary by source. Some endpoints have limited event counts. Absolute risks are sensitive to these differences. Calibration and Brier measures describe the measured risk transport.
7. The recurrence subset in GSE31210 uses the original GEO relapse annotation. Analysis of recurrence or clinical interventions requires harmonised outcomes and independent confirmation.
